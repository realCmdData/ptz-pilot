# Changelog

Version numbers follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the first number changes when
something works differently than before, the second when features are added, the third for fixes.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Move the camera with on-screen buttons, the keyboard, by clicking the picture, or by scrolling to zoom.
- Save camera positions and recall them with the number keys.
- Follow a person with a wide, medium or close shot, or a tight "Face only" close-up that predicts
  where the face is heading. Automatic zoom can be off, gentle, normal or quick.
- Park the camera and switch the picture off when no app is using it, and come back when it is needed.
- Send the picture to OBS Virtual Camera, with or without tracking boxes, or open it as a local
  browser link for an OBS Browser Source.
- Change the movement, zoom and Follow me keys, and optionally use them while another app is in front.
- Start with Windows, minimized.
